SmartSafe Slave — final alignment with the updated Master

1. Added green LED on PD6; red LED remains on PD7; buzzer remains on PA3.
2. USART is receive-only at 9600 baud, 8N1.
3. Accepted Master messages are compared exactly:
   LOGIN_OK, FAILURE, BREACH, Tamper, POWER_FAIL,
   POWER RESTORED, WDT_RESET.
4. LOGIN_OK turns the green LED on for 5 seconds.
5. FAILURE, BREACH and Tamper turn the red LED and buzzer on for 5 seconds.
6. POWER_FAIL starts a persistent red LED/buzzer alarm.
7. POWER RESTORED is the only message that clears the persistent power alarm.
8. Slave LCD displays user-friendly two-line status text.
9. Log-dump sections between "=== LOG DUMP ===" and "=== END ===" are ignored.
10. Unrecognized lines such as LOG CLEARED are ignored.
11. Replaced substring matching with exact strcmp() checks to stop log lines
    from falsely triggering alarms.
12. Added overflow-safe line reception: overlong lines are discarded until CR/LF.
13. Replaced <util/delay.h> with Timer0 polling delay_ms()/delay_us().
14. Timer2 initialization now stops, resets and clears stale flags before start.
15. Watchdog remains enabled with a 2-second timeout.

Hardware map:
  LCD data PC0..PC7; RS PA0; EN PA2; R/W tied to GND.
  Buzzer PA3.
  Green LED PD6 through a current-limiting resistor.
  Red LED PD7 through a current-limiting resistor.
  Master PD1/TXD -> Slave PD0/RXD.
  Master and Slave grounds must be common.
