SmartSafe Master — applied power/sleep/timing changes

1. Confirmed POWER_FAIL behavior
   - Send POWER_FAIL and wait until the final USART stop bit is transmitted.
   - Log EVT_POWER_FAIL to the external SPI EEPROM.
   - Disable Timer0/1/2, timer interrupts, INT0, ADC, SPI, USART, and
     watchdog. The Master has no local LED or buzzer outputs.
   - Enter Idle sleep with the analog comparator interrupt as the only active
     interrupt source.
   - When the monitored divider voltage is restored, reinitialize the disabled
     peripherals, raise one POWER_OK event, and return to normal operation.

2. Inactivity sleep
   - Remains SLEEP_MODE_PWR_DOWN.
   - INT0 is configured as a low-level wake source immediately before sleep.
   - Comparator interrupt is disabled during inactivity sleep, so INT0 is the
     intended wake source.
   - After INT0 wake, the comparator is re-enabled and its current state is
     resynchronized.

3. Timing correctness
   - Added timer_get_seconds() for atomic 32-bit timestamp reads.
   - All direct main.c reads of system_time_s were replaced.
   - Timer0 restoration now stops the timer first, clears OCF0, restores OCR0,
     TCNT0 and TIMSK, then restores TCCR0 last.

4. Startup failure
   - If the monitored voltage is already low at startup, one POWER_FAIL event
     is generated and the Master enters comparator-only standby.
