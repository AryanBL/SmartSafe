Master LCD and UI-hold update
================================

1. The Master LCD now displays only:
   - Enter Password:
   - One * for every entered digit on row 2

2. Removed Master LCD event/status messages, including startup splash, access
   result, tamper warning, wrong-password warning, and lockout text. The Slave
   owns all event/status display.

3. ui_hold_until remains as an input-inhibit timer only. During a hold:
   - keypad input is blocked;
   - inactivity sleep is blocked;
   - the Master LCD is not changed.

4. activity_timer is reset when a hold starts and again when it ends, giving
   the user a complete 10-second active period after keypad input resumes.

5. activity_timer is also reset when a 30-second lockout ends, preventing an
   immediate inactivity sleep after lockout release.
